# Elven Steel

The Design Docs and Outline for the "Elven Steel" Sci-Fi Gaelic-language WebTV series set in Parallel-Earth

### Basic Premise

The story is set to be spoken entirely in Irish Gaelic, and is centered around a robot elf character who initially starts out with the name E.Lo.Di.E., comes to be called "Elly", and then later called Dælinor (an elvish word for "sword-wielding"). She begins aboard an alien spacecraft in orbit around a version of Earth in a parallel dimension on a parallel timeline. This parallel version of Earth is occupied by elves who had escaped from our version of Earth via an interdimensional version of a "Stargate". 

The elves living on this version of Earth are all organic. They are alive and carbon-based. Elly is the only robot, and the story centers around her. She initially starts out as a lone mechanic who loops through her daily routine aboard a destroyed alien spacecraft. All of the aliens have either been killed or have fled into the far reaches of outer space. One day, which is a moment that's depicted in the pilot episode, Elly views a video transmission feed of elves that are planetside. She recognizes the living, flesh, carbon-based elves as looking like her, and interprets them as being the people who are the basis of her build. Initially feeling as though she would prefer to stay aboard the ship, she sees no actual reason to stay, and decides to take a one-way ship down to the planet, as there is no way to get back up into orbit around outer space. At least, it's not immediately obvious that there is. 

She becomes friends with the elves living planetside. She soon learns that the elves are in conflict with a local race of orcs. Both elves and orcs were genetically engineered by the aliens, who are now either dead or long gone. Over the course of her time planetside, she gradually learns sword fighting, and becomes a self-taught samurai. Though, this requires extensive training, and she makes many, many mistakes along the way. This is made somewhat easier, because one of the elves is in a relationship with a human boyfriend, who somehow accidentally stumbled through one of the very last remaining interdimensional stargates, and stumbled into this world populated by escapee elves. The human is an Irishman, who has no knowledge of sword fighting, has no knowledge of martial arts, but he is a gigantic weeb. And so, while hiking through the hills of Ireland one day, he was carrying a pocketbook copy of Miyamoto Musashi's "The Book of Five Rings". Elly initially practices with a wooden sword, and later with a metal sword, and ultimately, one of the elf blacksmiths manages to craft a sword from a broken shard of the metal personal spacecraft that she crashed to Earth from, from the alien spaceship.

So, she is able to practice sword fighting and swordwork, although this requires a great deal of effort, concentration, and practice from her. She is helped by the fact that she has experience as a space-born mechanic. However, it's unfamiliar to her, and she makes many, many mistakes along the way, and constantly has to fine-tune her technique and her skills. She tries to learn from her mistakes, and slowly, gradually gets better throughout her training. However, the lessons that she learns throughout the course of the story are actually in the "Hagakure" book. The book does not actually appear in the TV show, it's nowhere to be found. It's just that the philosophical lessons that are contained within Hagakure are lessons that she needs to learn through hardship and struggle over the course of her personal story.

Other characters in the show include the organic living elves, Selandriel Lumorndaer and Rhylanna Aleanaear, among others. Selandriel is a peaceful, serene elf who is in a meaningful long-term relationship with the human man. Rhylanna is a bright, bubbly personality who turns out to be Ellie's best friend, although Elly can appear rather cold and distant, because she doesn't really know how to handle emotions and connections with living entities, as the aliens who created her either were wiped out or fled shortly before she came online.

The final battle of the show involves Elly taking responsibility not only for herself, but for her newfound family and tribe, and fighting back against the orcs. At the end, Elly sacrifices herself so that her fam- her family and tribe can make it into outer space, and later end up colonizing the parallel-dimension, parallel-timeline version of Mars.
