Locations named in the premise (`../README.md`). Details are open.

**The alien spacecraft:** destroyed, in orbit around the parallel Earth. Elly loops her daily routine aboard it as its lone mechanic. Its aliens were killed or fled into the far reaches of space.

**The one-way ship:** the small personal craft Elly takes down to the planet. There is no obvious way back up into orbit. It crashes, and a shard of its metal becomes her sword.

**The parallel Earth:** a version of Earth in a parallel dimension and on a parallel timeline.
- **The elves' lands:** home of the organic, carbon-based elves who escaped from our Earth.
- **The orcs' lands:** home of the race the elves are in conflict with.
- **The elf blacksmith's forge:** where Elly's sword is made.

**The orc-held compound:** a compound the orcs have taken over, holding a technology that can get people off the planet. The orcs haven't worked out how to operate it, but they are dangerously close. It is the finale's way into space.

**The stargates:** interdimensional gates between our Earth and the parallel Earth. The elves escaped through one; only a very few still work.

**Our Earth, the hills of Ireland:** where the Irishman was hiking when he stumbled through one of the last gates.

**The parallel Mars:** where Elly's family and tribe eventually settle, after her sacrifice lets them reach space.
