The full premise, in the author's own words, is in `../README.md`. This is a summary of it.

**Format:** a sci-fi WebTV series, spoken entirely in Irish Gaelic.

**Setting:** a version of Earth in a parallel dimension and on a parallel timeline. It is occupied by elves who escaped from our Earth through an interdimensional "stargate". Why they left is open; the author's original thought was increasing hostility from humans.

**Lead:** E.Lo.Di.E., later called Elly, and later Dælinor (elvish for "sword-wielding"). She is a robot elf and the only robot in the story. She starts as a lone mechanic looping her daily routine aboard a destroyed alien spacecraft in orbit; its aliens were killed or fled shortly before she came online.

**Pilot:** Elly sees a video feed of the elves planetside, recognizes them as the basis of her build, and takes a one-way ship down to the planet.

**Story:**
- **The world below:** the elves are in conflict with a local race of orcs. Both races were genetically engineered by the aliens.
- **Her training:** Elly teaches herself the sword, from a wooden sword, to a metal one, to a sword an elf blacksmith forges from a shard of the ship she crashed in. She makes many mistakes along the way.
- **Her teacher's book:** Selandriel's Irish boyfriend stumbled through one of the last stargates while hiking in Ireland, carrying Musashi's *The Book of Five Rings*.
- **Her real lessons** are those of *Hagakure*, which never appears in the show.

**The way into space:** a technology on the parallel Earth, inside a compound the orcs have taken over. The orcs haven't worked out how to operate it, but they are dangerously close.

**Ending:** Elly takes responsibility for her new family and tribe and fights back against the orcs. She sacrifices herself so that they can reach outer space; they later colonize the parallel world's Mars.

**Seed:** the 2023 short-story idea *Shardfall* (`media/Literature/short stories/Elven Steel - Shardfall/`).
